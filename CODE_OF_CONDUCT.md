# Code of Conduct — Icon Sync

## Our Commitment

We are committed to keeping **Icon Sync** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free open-source community for everyone—regardless of experience level, background, identity, or device ecosystem.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and constructive intent in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and focus technical critique on code, architecture, and user impact rather than individuals.
- Credit contributors accurately and uphold the project's **GNU AGPL v3.0 or later** open-source principles.
- Follow responsible disclosure steps in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) rather than posting active exploits publicly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, slurs, trolling, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit consent.
- Intentional disruption of discussions, spam, or bad-faith PRs.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close comments, commits, or issues that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat or severe offenders from project spaces.

To report an incident privately, contact the repository maintainer directly via the contact channel listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
